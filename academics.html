Academics

Our Learning Journey

From Creche to Junior Secondary – a continuous path of growth guided by faith, discipline and academic excellence.

👶

Creche

A warm, secure and stimulating space for babies and toddlers. Focus on care, early sensory play, language exposure and building trust.

🧒

Nursery

Play-based learning that develops social skills, basic literacy, numeracy, creativity and early Islamic knowledge through songs, stories and activities.

📘

Primary

Solid foundation in English, Mathematics, Basic Science, Social Studies, Islamic Religious Studies, Qur’an, Arabic and creative arts. Character and discipline are emphasised.

🎓

Junior Secondary (JSS)

Preparation for senior secondary with a broader curriculum, stronger Islamic studies, and focus on critical thinking, leadership and responsibility.

What We Teach

A balanced blend of Islamic and modern subjects.

📖

Islamic Studies & Qur’an

Qur’anic recitation, memorisation, Hadith, Fiqh basics, Seerah and good character (Akhlaq).

🔤

English & Literacy

Reading, writing, comprehension, grammar and confident communication skills.

🔢

Mathematics

Number sense, problem solving, reasoning and everyday application of maths.

🔬

Science & Social Studies

Curiosity about the world, basic scientific thinking and understanding of community and society.

🎨

Creative Arts & Skills

Drawing, crafts, music, drama and practical life skills that build confidence and creativity.

💻

Computer Studies

Introduction to digital literacy and responsible use of technology (taught by our Computer Studies Teacher).

Admissions

We welcome new pupils into Creche, Nursery, Primary and JSS. For current admission status, fees and registration, please contact the school office or visit the Tespire portal.
