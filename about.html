Who We Are

FOMWAN Basic School is owned and managed by the Federation of Muslim Women’s Associations in Nigeria (FOMWAN) FCT Chapter.

Located at Plot 190, Aminu Kano Crescent, Wuse 2, Abuja, the school provides a serene and conducive learning environment for children from Creche through Nursery, Primary and Junior Secondary levels.

We believe education is both a right and a religious duty. Our mission is to raise children who are academically sound, morally upright and proud ambassadors of Islam.

Our Vision

About FOMWAN

The Federation of Muslim Women’s Associations in Nigeria (FOMWAN) is a non-profit, non-governmental organisation established in 1985. It is the largest Muslim women’s umbrella body in Nigeria with chapters in all 36 states and the FCT.

FOMWAN’s five cardinal principles cover Da’wah, Education, Health, Humanitarian Services, and Youth & Women Development. Education is one of its strongest pillars – the organisation owns and supports many schools across Nigeria.

Our school in Wuse 2 is part of this noble educational mission.
